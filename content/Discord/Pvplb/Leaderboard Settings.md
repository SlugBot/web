## team0SizeMin
Minimum players on team 0
Default: 1
### team0SizeMax
Maximum players per team 0
Default: 1
### team1SizeMin
Minimum players on team 1
Default: 1
### team1SizeMax
Maximum players on team 1
Default: 1
### maxReserves
Maximum number of reserves in a comp team
Default: 0
### rankByPlayer
Rank players individually or rank the team (1 = rank individually, 0 = rank team)
Default: 1
### challengeTimeout
Amount of time, in minutes, before an unaccepted challenge will timeout.
Default: 360
### matchTimeout
Amount of time, in minutes, before an accepted challenge will timeout.
Default: 120
### timeBeforeCleanup
Amount of time, in minutes, before a completed challenge message is deleted. (set to -1 for no clean-up)
Default: 1440
### automatch
When 1, compatible challenges will be automatically matched. (Set to 0 to disable)
Default: 1
### allowPlatformLimit
Allow players to set platform limits in their challenges (Set to 0 to disable)
Default: 0
### ladderReachUp
The number of ranks a member can challenge above their own. (set to -1 for unlimited reach)
Default: -1
### ladderReachDown
The number of ranks a member can challenge below their own. (set to -1 for unlimited reach)
Default: -1
### dailyMatchLimit
Max number of matches a player can have per day. (set to -1 for unlimited daily matches)
Default: -1
### maxRounds
Number of rounds to consider for [[Point Settings#pointsPerRound|pointsPerRound]]
Default: 5
### readOnly
Disables the ability for users to put out challenges on this leaderboard (set to 1 for readOnly)
Default: 0
