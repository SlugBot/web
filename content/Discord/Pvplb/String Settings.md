### lbDescription
String shown at top of the leaderboard embed.
#### default
```
Challenge other members with \`/challenge someone\`. Alternatively, use \`/challenge anyone\` to put out an open challenge anyone can accept. To accept a challenge click the \`Accept Challenge\` button under the challenge notification.

Remember to declare the outcome with
\`/iwon <your score> <their score>\`
or
\`/ilost <your score> <their score>\`
after you've completed the match.
```
